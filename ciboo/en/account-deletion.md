# Account and Data Deletion Procedure - Ciboo

This documentation explains how to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data for the **Ciboo** application, developed by **Intelligence Industrielle**.

## 1. How to request account deletion

You have two options to delete your account and associated data:

### Option A: Within the Application (Recommended)
The fastest way to delete your account is to use the built-in feature:
1.  Open the **Ciboo** app.
2.  Go to the **Settings** screen (gear icon).
3.  Select your **Profile** or account options.
4.  Tap **Delete my account**.
5.  Confirm your choice in the security dialog.

Once confirmed, your account is immediately deactivated and your data is queued for permanent deletion.

### Option B: Request by Email
If you have uninstalled the app or no longer have access to it, you can request deletion by email:
*   **Recipient**: [support@intelligenceindustrielle.com]
*   **Subject**: Ciboo Account Deletion Request
*   **Message**: Please include the email address associated with your account so we can identify and process the deletion.

## 2. Types of data deleted or retained

### Deleted Data
Upon deleting your account, we permanently erase:
*   **User Profile**: Name, email address, and profile picture.
*   **Activity Data**: Grocery lists, product inventories, and personalized preferences.

### Retained Data
For legal compliance and security reasons, we retain certain information:
*   **Billing History**: If you have an active or past subscription, invoices and receipts are kept for **7 years** in accordance with applicable tax laws.
*   **Anonymized Security Data**: Anonymous access logs may be kept temporarily (maximum 180 days) to prevent abuse and ensure system security.
*   **Statistical Data**: Aggregated and anonymized usage statistics (which do not identify you) are kept for service improvement.

## 3. Additional Retention Period
For requests made through the app, logical deletion is immediate. Physical deletion from all backups may take up to **30 days**.
