# **Privacy Policy of Ciboo**

*Last updated: March 27, 2026*

The **Ciboo** application (hereinafter "the Application") is published by Intelligence Industrielle Inc. (hereinafter "We" or "Us"). Respecting your privacy and securing your personal data are at the core of our priorities.

This Privacy Policy aims to inform you about how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use Ciboo.

---

## **1. Data Collected and Purpose**

To provide you with our culinary assistant and grocery list creation services, we collect a minimum amount of data:

- **Account Information:** Upon registration, we collect your **name** and **email address**. These data are used exclusively to identify your account, secure your connection (via our authentication provider), and contact you if necessary.
- **Unique Identifiers (User ID):** A unique identifier is assigned to you to securely link your dietary preferences, favorite recipes, and grocery lists to your account.
- **Device and Usage Information:** The Application may collect information such as your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, the time spent on the Application, and the operating system used, in order to analyze and improve our services.

## **2. Specific Authorizations and Permissions**

To enhance your experience, Ciboo may ask for access to certain features of your device. **These accesses are never used without your knowledge.**

### **A. Microphone Access**

The Application requires access to your microphone **solely** to allow you to use voice input (for example, dictating your preferences or adding items to your grocery list).

- **What we do with it:** The audio stream is temporarily processed to be transcribed into text.
- **What we don't do:** No voice recording is kept, stored long-term, or listened to by humans. The audio file is deleted immediately after its textual transcription.

### **B. Location (Optional)**

The Application has a feature allowing you to indicate your geographical area (e.g., "Quebec").

- **Why?** This information allows us to adapt the Artificial Intelligence's recommendations in order to offer you dishes based on seasonal ingredients available in your region.
- **Optional nature:** This information is strictly **declarative and optional**. Ciboo **does not track** your background GPS position. You can modify or delete this location at any time in your Settings. If you do not provide it, the Application will function normally with standard recommendations.

## **3. Data Sharing and Security**

**We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for commercial or advertising purposes.**

Your data (profile, dietary preferences) are hosted on secure servers. We use third-party infrastructure services (such as Supabase for authentication and databases) that adhere to strict security standards.

Textual interactions with the Artificial Intelligence are processed anonymously for the exclusive purpose of generating your meal plans and recipe algorithms.

## **4. Your Rights and Data Deletion**

In accordance with applicable laws on personal data protection, you have the right to access, rectify, and delete your data.

Within the Application, via the "Settings" screen, you can at any time:

- Request the **complete deletion of your account** and associated data.
- Reset or delete your preferences, history, and grocery lists in a single click.

You can also stop all basic information collection by the Application easily by uninstalling it.

## **5. Children**

We do not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@intelligenceindustrielle.com so that we can take the necessary actions to remove it from our servers.
